            How the race works

            Each opponent uses its top Limitless Champions ability and item, every damaging move on at least 5% of its sets, and its top Smogon ladder spread (or a max-offense preset). Each side uses the move that KOs in the fewest hits with at least a 50% chance; fewer hits wins, and equal hits go to whoever moves first. This is a one-on-one measure, not a doubles win rate: partners, Protect, switching, accuracy and secondary effects are left out.
