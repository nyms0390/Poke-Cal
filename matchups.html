    How the race works

    Each opponent uses its top Limitless Champions ability and item, every damaging move on at least 5% of its sets, and its top Smogon ladder spread (or a max-offense preset). Each side uses the move that KOs in the fewest turns, counting a hit once its KO chance reaches 50%; fewer turns wins, and equal turns go to whoever moves first. Charge, recharge and delayed moves add their extra turns, Fake Out and Explosion-style moves count only as a first-turn KO, and conditional moves such as Last Resort or Focus Punch race as if their condition holds. This is a one-on-one measure, not a doubles win rate: partners, Protect, switching, accuracy and secondary effects are left out.

      Beyond the usual sets

      —

      Every Champions Pokémon, not only the top 50 or 100, checked with full-investment theory sets: abilities, plausible items and its strongest moves into your types.

      Popular Pokémon, rare choices

      Pokémon with at least 20 Limitless teams whose usual set does not beat you, but a set one or two rare choices away does. A rare choice is an ability, item, nature or KO move on fewer than 5% of their Limitless sets. Popular Pokémon outside your top list whose usual set already beats you are listed too.

        Rarely used Pokémon

        Pokémon with fewer than 20 Limitless teams, too few to tell rare choices from common ones. Each gets its strongest theory set; only the ways of beating you that nothing above already covers are listed, hard counters first.
